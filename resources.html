    App we're watching

    Guava Health.

    A personal health tracker app that brings your medical records, wearables and day-to-day logs into one place.

    • Pulls records from connected patient portals (Guava lists 40,000+ US providers) and lets you upload paper records and scans.
    • Syncs with wearables and devices such as Apple Health, Fitbit, Garmin, Oura and WHOOP, plus some glucose monitors.
    • Logs symptoms, medications and mood, with medication reminders.
    • Lets you share your profile with your doctor, family or a carer.
    • Free to start, with a paid Premium plan for extra features such as reading details from uploaded documents.

    Provider connections are mainly US-based; check what works in Australia before you sign up. Description summarised from guavahealth.com and Guava's own video.
