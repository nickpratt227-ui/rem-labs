How to size a watch band: lug width and wrist size

1. Find the lug width

Lug width is the gap between the two lugs where the band attaches, in millimetres. Check your watch's manual or spec page, or measure it with a ruler or calipers. Common sizes are 18, 20, 22, 24 and 26mm.

2. Check the attachment

Look at the back of your current band. A small lever on the pin means quick-release. A latch on the band itself means a proprietary system, such as Garmin QuickFit or Fitbit's snap-in connectors, and you need a band made for that system.

3. Measure your wrist

Wrap a soft tape or a strip of paper around your wrist where you wear the watch, then measure it flat. Choose a band whose listed range includes your size with a little room to spare.

4. Similar names, different sizes

Some similar model names take different widths. For example, the Suunto 9 Peak takes 22mm but the original Suunto 9 takes 24mm. Always check the exact model.
