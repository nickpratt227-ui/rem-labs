Garmin replacement bands in Australia: a sizing and buying guide

Most Garmin watches take one of a few standard band widths. Get the width right and almost any quality band will fit. Get it wrong and nothing will.

QuickFit or standard pins?

Garmin's Fenix, Epix, Forerunner 9xx and Instinct ranges use QuickFit, a tool-free latch in 20, 22 or 26mm. Forerunner, Venu and Vivoactive models mostly use standard quick-release spring pins in 18, 20 or 22mm.

Check your model's manual or measure the gap between the lugs to confirm. Our sizing guide shows how.

Which material?

Silicone is easy to rinse and suits training and swimming. Nylon loops are light, breathable and comfortable to sleep in. Stretch loops slip on with no buckle but need the right wrist size.

Buying in Australia

Genuine Garmin bands cost more. Compatible third-party bands cost less and are widely sold. Look for an Australian seller with clear returns and sizes listed per model.
